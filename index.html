Haoran Sun

I am Haoran Sun, a researcher at Tencent Hy RL Team, working on scientific intelligence and large language models. My work focuses on training, alignment, and evaluation for STEM agents and STEM reasoning. I believe that agents will become deeply involved in scientific and engineering workflows—from chip design and aerospace engineering to the search for extraterrestrial civilizations—as we move toward artificial superintelligence (ASI).

01

What I do

I was a master's student at Tianjin University from 2022 to 2025, working in the TJUNLP Lab under the supervision of Prof. Deyi Xiong. My graduate research focused on reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) for large language models, fine-tuning large language models, multilingual neural machine translation, and end-to-end speech translation.

I am now a researcher at Tencent Hy RL Team, studying the capabilities of large language models in STEM, especially STEM agents and STEM reasoning. My work centers on reinforcement learning (RL), synthetic data generation, and supervised fine-tuning (SFT), with major contributions to capability development for Hy-series models (including Hy3 and Hy4), particularly the capabilities evaluated by Terminal-Bench-Science, Terminal-Bench-4, HLE, SUPERChem, etc.
